    What's new

    • Straighten and Reshape in Edit photo: drag four corners onto a worksheet or poster photographed at an angle to flatten it, or push, grow and shrink parts of a picture. Filters has Highlights, Shadows, Sharpen and Smooth grain.
    • Photo layers: Cut out now hides instead of deleting, with Erase and Bring back brushes. Adjust this part brightens or warms just what you choose, Fade melts a picture into the page, and Blend (under Transparency) mixes it with what's underneath. Layers shows each picture and can rename it.
    • ✂ Cut out on a picture: Remove the background in one click, or choose what to keep with the Smart brush, a box, the Magic wand, a lasso or a brush. Keep only that part, erase it, or make a sticker of it.
    • Remove things from any photo: Edit a photo on the start screen, or ✨ Retouch on a picture in a design. Paint over what you want gone. Photos' heal, clone and skin brushes are there too.
    • 📏 Rulers in the top bar: drag from a ruler to add a guide line things snap to. ▦ All pages shows every page at once; drag one to move it.
