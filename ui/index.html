    What's new

    • In Photos, everything has a button now: Pick, Reject and stars above the photos (or on each photo in the grid), Grid / Photo / Compare / Develop, arrows and a filmstrip, and in Develop: hold for before, Undo, Fit and 100%. Develop's settings are in drawers on the left, with each look shown as a picture.
    • Folders for your designs on the start screen: drag a design onto a folder, or use the ⋯ on a design. Deleted designs wait in the Trash.
    • A File menu next to Start: new design, make a copy, rename, folder, download and more.
    • In Elements: many more shapes, frames shaped like an arch, heart or star, and photo grids that fill the page with frames.
    • Font combinations in Text: ready-made pairings to click or drag in.
