# Changelog

## 0.25.0

- My elements, at the top of Elements: select anything on the page (a decoration from a Canva file, a styled heading, a chart, a few pieces together) and click Save the selection to My elements, or Save to My elements in the ⋯ and right-click menus. It waits there, with a picture of it, for any design.
- Click a saved element to add it, or drag it onto the page. Several pieces saved together come back loose and the same distance apart; a saved table comes back as its own table.
- ✎ renames a saved element; × asks once, then deletes it.
- Saved elements are kept as files in KB Studio/Elements, next to your designs, so they're backed up with them.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.24.0

- Tables can be changed after they're added: click any cell and use Table in the toolbar to add a row above or below, a column left or right, or delete a row or column. New rows make the table taller; new columns share the table's width.
- Each cell can have its own colour (No colour clears it), and the borders' colour and thickness change for the whole table at once.
- A row grows taller when its words no longer fit, as in Canva.
- Select the whole table, to move or resize it in one go, and Delete the table.
- Tables from Canva files can be changed the same way, merged cells included.
- Text alignment is one button now, as in Canva: each click moves from left to centre, right and justified. It frees room in the toolbar.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.23.0

- Gradients in Colours: twelve ready-made ones (soft pinks, lavenders and blues among them) and a maker with two colours, a ⇄ swap and a direction (down, across, corner to corner or from the middle). They work on text, shapes and the page itself, and opening Colours on a gradient shows how it was made so it can be changed.
- Set as background: a photo's toolbar (and its ⋯ menu) can make it fill the page behind everything, locked in place so it doesn't move while you work on top. Unlock it to move or resize it again.
- See-through PNG: tick it under Download to leave out the page colour, for stickers, logos and anything you'll lay over something else.
- Table and QR code in Elements fold away like the other sections.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.22.0

- Charts in Elements: column, bar, line, pie and donut. Click one to add it, or drag it onto the page.
- Edit chart in the toolbar: change the kind, add a title, and type a label, number and colour for each row. + Add a row and × take rows away; Show the numbers turns the values on the chart on or off. The chart keeps its place and size as it changes.
- Charts in Canva files now come across as charts you can edit, with their labels, numbers, colours and title. A chart with several series brings the first one, and the import note says so.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.21.0

- Decorations in Elements, drawn for KB Studio: maple, oak and autumn leaves, falling leaves, acorn, pumpkin, wheat, apple and mushroom; leafy sprigs, eucalyptus, fern, a branch, a leaf wreath and leafy corners; daisy, blossom, tulip, rose, lavender, sunflower, a bouquet, a floral corner and a flower border; sparkles, a doodle heart, a swirly arrow, squiggles, burst lines, a circle scribble, an underline swash, a boho rainbow, a sun, a cloud and a star; flourish, dotted and leafy dividers, a corner flourish and a scalloped label; confetti, balloons, bunting, a ribbon banner and a bow; and a pencil, camera, paw print and books.
- Any decoration's colours show as dots in the toolbar: click one to change that colour everywhere in it. Ungroup a decoration to move its pieces apart.
- The Elements sections (Shapes, Frames, Photo grids, Decorations, Graphics) fold away with a click on their heading, and stay the way you left them.
- Curved text: Effects has a Curve slider, from a smile to an arch. The text goes straight while you type in it and curves again after.
- Bulleted and numbered lists (click List once for bullets, twice for numbers, a third time for none); new lines get their bullet or number when you click away.
- Capitals (aA) and strikethrough for text. Turning capitals off brings back the letters as you typed them.
- Copy style: select something, click Copy style, then click what should look the same. Text takes the font, size, colours, effect and curve; shapes take the fill, outline, corners and shadow.
- Position has Size and rotation: type an exact width, height or angle, with Keep proportions on or off.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.20.0

- A new light look across the whole app: soft blush and lavender panels, white cards, orchid and pink-to-purple buttons, and light blue touches.
- The start screen has the KB Studio artwork as its background, a welcome card, and rounded cards for Photos, Create and Import from Canva.
- Photos, the grid, the filmstrip and Develop keep a neutral grey behind every photo, so colours and exposure are judged fairly against the lighter app around them.
- In Create the page sits on a soft lavender pasteboard, and the snapping guides are pink.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.19.0

- Photos works with the mouse alone. A bar above the photos has Pick, Reject, Unflag and one to five stars for the current photo (click the same star again to clear it), Copy settings and Paste settings, and in the grid Select all.
- Grid, Photo, Compare and Develop buttons in the header switch views, with no keys needed.
- In the grid, pointing at a photo shows a small flag and star bar on it, so photos can be rated without opening them; ratings stay shown on the photo.
- Arrows over the large photo and over Develop go to the previous and next photo, and a filmstrip under them shows every photo in the filter: click one to go to it.
- Develop's bar has Hold for before (keep the button pressed to see the photo without your edits), Undo, Redo, and zoom: −, Fit, 100% and +.
- Develop's settings moved to the left, in drawers like Create's: Adjust, Looks, Crop and Retouch. Looks shows each look as a small picture of the photo you're editing; click one to use it. R and the brush keys open their drawer.
- The header stays on one line, so long messages no longer push the photo down.
- On Zorin and other GNOME-style desktops the window has minimize and maximize buttons again, next to close. The app now asks the desktop which title bar buttons it uses (as an AppImage it couldn't read that setting, so it showed close alone).
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.18.0

- Folders for designs on the start screen, under Your designs: All designs, your folders, and + New folder. Drag a design onto a folder to move it there, or use the ⋯ on a design (or right-click it) to open, make a copy, rename, move to a folder or move to the trash. Folders can be renamed or deleted; deleting one keeps its designs.
- A Trash: designs moved there disappear from your lists but stay until you restore them or empty the trash.
- A File menu in Create, next to Start: create a new design, import from Canva, make a copy, rename, choose its folder, save as template, download and print, or move it to the trash. It also shows how many pages the design has and its size.
- More in Elements: 16 new shapes (oval, half circle, diamond, pentagon, hexagon, octagon, parallelogram, heart, 4- and 6-point stars, plus, block and double arrows, badge, banner, cloud), dashed and dotted lines, and a two-way arrow.
- New frame shapes: arch, oval, heart, star and hexagon. Pictures can take these shapes from the Frame menu too.
- Photo grids in Elements: seven layouts (two side by side, two stacked, three across, one big and two small, big top with three below, four and nine squares) that fill the page with frames, ready for photos.
- Font combinations in the Text drawer: twelve ready-made pairings, shown in their own fonts (Thank you, Mini sessions, Student of the week, Save the date, New gallery and more). Click or drag one onto the page; its lines arrive together and selected, and each stays its own text to edit.
- The What's new card appears once more, shorter, listing only the latest changes, and it scrolls on small windows instead of running off the top.

## 0.17.0

- Import from Canva, on the start screen. In Canva choose Share → Download → PowerPoint, then pick the file: every page comes across as an editable design, with its pictures (cropped and shaped as in Canva), text, shapes, tables, groups and colours, in the same page size.
- The fonts Canva puts inside the file come too, saved with the design and listed at the bottom of the font menu as "(in this design)". Fonts it didn't include are swapped for the closest bundled one, and a note lists the swaps and anything left out, such as charts or videos.
- A PNG or JPEG downloaded from Canva opens as a locked picture on a page of the same shape, ready for new text and photos on top.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.16.0

- Filters for pictures in Create: select a picture and click Filters for the same looks as Photos, and your own Develop presets, shown as previews of that picture. A strength slider tones a look down, and Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Warmth and Vignette adjust it further.
- The original picture is kept with the design, so a filter can be changed or removed at any time without losing quality. Cropping, frames and size stay put when a filter changes.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.15.0

- Colours, next to the colour box: the colours already used in this design (every page), saved colours and a default palette. With nothing selected it colours the background.
- Text effects under Effects: Shadow, Lift, Hollow, Outline and Neon, shown as previews. Recolouring hollow or neon text recolours its outline or glow. Effects are kept with the design.
- Drag layers in the Layers list to reorder them.
- The start screen shows a picture of each recent design's first page.

## 0.14.0

- Pages. A strip under the page shows them all; click one to edit it, add a blank page, duplicate, delete or move the one you're on. Undo covers page changes. PDF and Print include every page, once per copy; PNG and JPEG save a numbered picture per page.
- Resize scales everything on every page to fit the new size, kept centred, unless "Scale everything to fit" is unticked. Custom size takes any width and height from 1 to 36 inches.
- Designs with more than one page or a custom size are saved in format 2, which older KB Studio refuses with a message. One-page designs in a standard size are still saved as before.
- The What's new card appears once more.

## 0.13.0

- Photo frames in Elements: square, portrait, landscape, rounded and circle. Drop a photo on a frame, or on any picture, and it fills that spot, cropped from the middle, keeping the place, size, angle and shape. Dragging over one outlines it first. Clicking a photo, or uploading one, with an empty frame selected fills it.
- This shoot: a drawer with the photos of the shoot open in Photos, picks first and rejects left out. Click or drag one onto the page; it's developed with your edits at 2048 pixels.
- New sizes in Resize: an 11 × 17 poster; 4 × 6, 5 × 7 and 8 × 10 photo prints; Instagram posts, square posts and stories, which save at exactly 1080 × 1350, 1080 × 1080 and 1080 × 1920.
- The taskbar and app menu switch to the new icon straight after updating, instead of keeping the old one until the next login.
- Six templates with frames ready for photos: Student of the week, Mini session flyer, Photo card (5 × 7), Instagram post, Square collage and Story. Templates bring their own page size, and their pictures show that shape.

## 0.12.0

- Create shows grey space around the page. Things can hang off the edge, faded outside the page, and anything outside is left out of exports and prints.
- Scroll moves around the page and Shift+scroll moves sideways; Ctrl+scroll zooms where the pointer is. Hold Space or the middle mouse button and drag to pan. The page can't be scrolled out of sight, and Fit, or 0, shows it all again.
- Drag shapes, text and graphics from the drawers onto the page and they land where they're dropped. Dropped pictures land where they're dropped too.
- Resizing shows the size in inches and rotating shows the angle. Rotation snaps near 45°; hold Shift to snap in 15° steps. Hovering outlines what will be selected.
- The What's new card appears once more with the new tips.

## 0.11.0

- Create has a Canva-style layout. Icon buttons down the left open drawers for Templates, Elements, Text, Uploads, Draw and Layers; the toolbar along the top shows only the options for what is selected; Download & print and Resize sit in the top bar. A one-time card explains the new layout.
- A floating toolbar above the selection duplicates, locks and deletes. Right-click, or ⋯, opens a menu with copy, paste, duplicate, delete, bring to front, forward, backward, send to back, lock, group and centre on the page.

- A new icon: a camera with an apple in its lens and a gold star.
- KB Studio adds itself to the app menu at launch, replacing a hand-made menu entry, and on GNOME (Zorin OS) pins itself to the taskbar once. Updates refresh the entry and icon.

## 0.10.0

- Retouch brushes: Blur (L), Dodge (O) and Burn (I). Dodge and burn start at 25% strength. Recipes move to version 6; older KB Studio can't open them.

## 0.9.0

- Create editing: smart guides and page snapping; group and ungroup; align selected objects and distribute equal gaps; cut, copy, paste and duplicate selections across designs; paste or drop pictures; zoom and Fit.
- More shape and text properties, picture frames, triangles, stars, rounded rectangles, speech bubbles and free drawing. A 0.25-inch print-safe overlay stays out of exports.
- Switching designs waits for pending imports and pastes to save; layer shortcuts preserve selection order at stack boundaries. Native file drops use the HTML importer.
- Updating waits for design navigation and pending additions to save, and keeps editing frozen until restart or update failure. A failed design open does not cancel the update.
- Undo history shares embedded image data between snapshots instead of repeating it.
- Crop pictures directly, with Apply and Cancel, using Fabric 7.4. Frames resize with the crop, and finishing or cancelling restores zoom and scrolling. Existing designs keep their left/top origins and format 1.
- Create content: the full Twemoji 15.0.0 bundle with searchable categories and paged results; six more fonts; certificate, name-tag, flash-card, calendar and handwriting templates; editable tables, handwriting lines and vector QR codes. Designs and starters open with a persistent fallback-font warning if fonts fail to load; exports and Print wait for all bundled fonts and refuse output after a font failure. Save and restart KB Studio to try loading fonts again; reinstall if the problem persists. Local clip-art requests are allowed by the packaged CSP; narrow table cells start with blank text.

## 0.8.0

- Detail in Develop: Sharpen (with Masking, so flat areas and noise aren't sharpened), Colour noise and Luminance noise reduction. Photos without Detail render exactly as before. Recipes move to version 5; older KB Studio can't open them.

## 0.7.0

- A start screen: Photos (as before) or Create. The app opens on it, unless it was launched with a shoot.
- Create: make posters, labels, charts and cards on a Letter, landscape Letter, half-sheet or quarter-sheet page. Add text, pictures, rectangles, circles, lines, arrows and clip art; set the background and colours; move, resize and rotate; reorder, hide and lock layers in the Layers list; align to the page; undo and redo (up to 50 steps). Pictures are shrunk to 3300 pixels on the long side.
- Designs save themselves, one file each, in `~/Documents/KB Studio/`, and the start screen lists the recent ones.
- Output: PDF at 300 dpi (one page per copy), PNG, JPEG, and Print, which makes the PDF and opens it in your PDF viewer. Half and quarter cards can be tiled two or four to a Letter sheet with cut marks. "Preview in B&W" shows how it prints on a black-and-white printer.
- Starter templates (behaviour chart, labels, word wall, newsletter) with placeholder words, and Save as template for your own.
- Five bundled fonts (Lexend, Andika, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Comic Neue, Fredoka) with favourites, saved colours, and 40 clip-art pictures (Twemoji, CC-BY 4.0).
- Use in a design: in Photos, sends the developed photo to a new page in Create at 2048 pixels.
- Create never loses work: a rename can't overwrite another design, a failed save keeps you on the page and says so, and closing the window or updating saves first (close again to quit anyway if it can't). The heal and clone preview also shows a source that is off-screen.

## 0.6.0

- Deep zoom in Develop: the wheel zooms at the pointer from fit up to 1600%, with + and - for steps, 0 to fit and 1 for 100%. A zoom readout and a navigator thumbnail with a draggable box show where you are. Above 200% pixels are drawn sharp, and the brush outline scales with the zoom. In Develop, 0 and 1 zoom instead of rating.
- Every retouch brush has a Strength slider. Soften's blur now follows the brush size, so a big brush softens wrinkles properly: spot heal at 100% removes them, 40-60% softens them. Photos retouched before keep their old look.
- Clone and heal: Alt+click sets the source (Ctrl+click still works), the brush shows a preview of what it will copy, and an Aligned option (on by default) keeps the same distance to the source between strokes.
- Renamed to KB Studio. The release file is now `KB.Studio_<version>_amd64.AppImage`; the updater replaces an installed copy in place, and settings and presets carry over.
- Recipes still open as before.

## 0.5.0

- Crop in Develop (R): drag the frame or its handles, choose an aspect (Original, Free, 1:1, 4:5, 5:7, 2:3, 16:9, or swap it to portrait), and straighten up to 45° either way; the frame keeps to the photo. Enter applies it as one undo step, Esc cancels.
- Deliver (Ctrl+E): finished JPEGs of the picks, the selection or everything except rejects, at full size or 2048 pixels for the web, with a quality setting. Files are named after the client and numbered in shoot order (Smith-Wedding-0001.jpg), or keep their own names. They go to `<shoot>/Delivered/<client>` or a folder you choose, with an optional PROOF watermark. The app asks once before replacing files already there, and the shoot remembers your choices.
- Clone and heal start each stroke from the spot you Ctrl+clicked, and show a line to the source while you paint.
- Recipes are now version 4; older ones still open.

## 0.4.0

- Looks in Develop: 18 of them in black & white, toned (sepia, warm brown, cool selenium) and colour (warm & soft, bright & airy, moody, golden hour, teal & orange, vintage and more), each with a 0–100% strength and every slider still working on top.
- Split toning for shadows and highlights; Finishing adds vignette, grain and fade.
- Presets: save the current settings under a name, apply them to any photo in any shoot, delete them. They live in the app's settings folder.
- Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and paste settings, onto the open photo or onto a grid selection (Ctrl+click, Shift+click, Ctrl+A; Esc clears).
- Red eye (E) in Retouch: click the pupil or drag a circle over the eye; the catchlight stays.
- Recipes are now version 3; older ones still open.

## 0.3.0

- Retouch in Develop: spot heal (J), heal from a source you choose (H), clone (S) and soften skin (B). Ctrl+click sets the heal or clone source; [ and ] change the brush size.
- Z zooms to 100% at the pointer and back; Space+drag or middle-drag moves around a zoomed photo. Esc puts the brush down before it leaves Develop.
- Strokes are saved in the recipe (now version 2; version 1 recipes still open) and replayed on the photo each time it opens, so the original is never changed. Ctrl+Z undoes a stroke.
- JPEGs are now decoded and edited at the same quality as RAW files, so retouching works on both.

## 0.2.0

- Develop: press D to edit a photo with white balance, exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, vibrance and saturation.
- RAW files are developed from their sensor data, so blown-looking highlights can often be brought back; JPEGs are edited directly.
- Hold \ to see the photo before your edits; Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo; double-click a slider to reset it, or use Reset all.
- Edits save as small recipes in `<shoot>/.shootstudio/recipes/`. The grid and loupe show edited photos; originals are never changed.

## 0.1.0

- Cull a shoot folder: RAW+JPEG pairs, thumbnail grid, loupe and side-by-side compare.
- Pick, reject and star from the keyboard; ratings save to `<shoot>/.shootstudio/shoot.json`, and originals are never changed.
- Checks for updates at launch and installs them with one click.
